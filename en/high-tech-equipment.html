Advanced Equipment
Introducing BOA Dental Clinic's advanced equipment.
3D Oral Scanner
Using a scanner instead of traditional impression materials,
impressions can be taken quickly and accurately.
3D Printer
With a highly precise printer and biocompatible materials,
3D printing is performed.
With dedicated in-house specialists,
BOA Dental Clinic offers more comfortable treatment.
Milling Machine
Prosthetics that used to be outsourced to a dental lab based on a traditional impression
are now custom-made in-house, tailored directly to each patient.

The directors, clinical staff, and lab staff all consult together to provide
precise treatment using better materials.
3D CT
Using state-of-the-art X-ray technology, a more accurate diagnosis
can be provided.
Waterlase Laser
By using laser technology for various treatments and surgeries,
patients can receive comfortable, low-bleeding, and virtually painless treatment.
Painless Anesthesia Device
Using a painless anesthesia device, a more comfortable anesthesia
can be provided.
Air-Flow
Using water and protein powder, even areas instruments can't reach
can be cleaned thoroughly and comfortably.
TMJ Laser
Through physical therapy such as low-level laser, infrared, and electrical stimulation,
jaw pain can be relieved.
