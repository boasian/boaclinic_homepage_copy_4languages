Digital Guided Implant— What is it?
Scanning the patient's bone and gum structure before surgery,
and using 3D virtual surgery
to determine the implant's
optimal position,
a surgical guide device is created
so the actual surgery can be performed exactly as simulated —

an implant surgery method.
Advantages of Digital Guided Implants
Precise Surgery
Short Surgery Time
With Minimal Incision,
swelling, bleeding, and pain
are minimized
Also suitable for patients with
chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension,
or for elderly
patients.
